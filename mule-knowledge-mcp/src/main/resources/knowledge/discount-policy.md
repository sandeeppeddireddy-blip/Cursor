# Discount and deal desk policy

Standard list pricing applies unless a discount is approved.

## Standard discounts

- Up to 10% for annual prepay on Professional edition.
- Up to 15% for multi-year (36-month) Enterprise agreements.
- Partner-sourced deals may add 5% partner margin, not stacked above 20% total off list.

## Deal desk

Discounts above 15% require Deal Desk approval. Submit the opportunity ID, competitive threat, and estimated close date in Salesforce. Deal Desk SLA is two business days.

Never quote a discount that is not recorded on the opportunity. Agentforce should summarize the policy and tell the seller to open a Deal Desk case for exceptions.
