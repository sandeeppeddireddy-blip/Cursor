# Product catalog

Internal catalog used by sales and support. Prices are list USD per user per month, billed annually unless noted.

## Northstar Cloud Professional

- SKU: NST-PRO
- List: $80
- Includes workflow automation, standard connectors, and 50,000 automation runs per month.
- Best for mid-market teams that need Salesforce and ERP sync.

## Northstar Cloud Enterprise

- SKU: NST-ENT
- List: $140
- Includes Professional features plus SSO, audit logs, sandbox, and unlimited automation runs.
- Required for customers with more than 500 employees or regulated data.

## Add-ons

- Knowledge Pack (SKU: NST-KNOW): $15. Adds managed knowledge search for Agentforce.
- Premium Support (SKU: NST-SUP): $8. 24x7 phone plus 1-hour P1 response.
