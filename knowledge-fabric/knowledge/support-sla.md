# Support SLA

Severity is assigned by Support, not by the customer’s requested priority.

## Response targets

- P1 (production outage): 1 hour, 24x7 for Premium Support SKUs; 4 hours business hours otherwise.
- P2 (major feature degraded): 4 business hours.
- P3 (workaround available): 1 business day.
- P4 (how-to / knowledge): 2 business days; prefer Agentforce plus this knowledge agent.

## Case routing

Cases tagged `agentforce` or `knowledge-gap` should cite the knowledge article ID. If no article matches, Support files a knowledge gap in the internal wiki.
