# Returns and cancellation policy

Customers may cancel unused annual subscriptions within 30 days of the order start date for a prorated refund. After 30 days, unused months are forfeited unless the contract includes a termination-for-convenience clause.

Implementation services are non-refundable once delivery has started.

Hardware kits (rare) must be returned unused within 14 days with RMA number from Support.

Seller talking point: do not promise refunds beyond this policy. Escalate legal exceptions to RevOps.
