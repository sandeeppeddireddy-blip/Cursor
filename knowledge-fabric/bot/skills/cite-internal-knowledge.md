# Cite internal knowledge

1. Search, then open the matching articles.
2. Write the answer only from those articles.
3. End with:

```
Answer: ...
Citations: <id> — <title>
Confidence: high | medium | low
Next step: ...
```

If the corpus does not contain the fact, Confidence is low and Next step is escalate. Do not fill gaps with general Salesforce or industry advice.
